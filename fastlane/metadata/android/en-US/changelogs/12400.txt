Tankoubons now behave like one book. A downloaded tankoubon follows the server's membership: members added later fold into its card, missing ones show as "Incomplete" with a start control. The card has the same progress bar, start/stop and multi-select actions as any download. Members have no separate detail page: tapping one opens the whole tankoubon at that member, and their history rows merge into one. In the drawer, tap a tankoubon to read it; long-press for download, members and delete.
