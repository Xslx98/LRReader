LR Reader is a feature-rich Android client for LANraragi, a self-hosted manga/archive management server.

Features:
• Full-featured search — keyword, category filter, sort, random, per-server search history
• High-performance reader — GL rendering, on-demand decoding and smart preloading
• Tankoubons — read a whole tankoubon as one book, with its own detail page, tags and one-tap download
• Offline download — background archive download with resume, read while downloading
• Progress sync — two-way reading progress with the server, kept per server
• Backup & restore — servers (without API keys), history, favourites, progress and settings to a file
• Tag translation — auto-translate tags in Chinese (EhTagDatabase)
• Archive rating — emoji-based star rating system
• Category management — browse, create, edit LANraragi categories
• Secure — API keys encrypted with the Android Keystore, pattern app lock, plain-HTTP servers marked
• Multi-server — configure and switch between multiple LANraragi instances
• Upload & URL download — add archives directly from the app
• Private by design — no analytics; crash reports stay on the device unless you share them
• Multi-language — 10 languages including CJK, English, French, German, Spanish, Thai
• Dark mode — follows system theme, AMOLED black

Built upon the EhViewer reading framework.
